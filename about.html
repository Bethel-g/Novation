About Nova

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Nova has grown to become a trusted partner for businesses seeking innovative technology solutions.

Nova Technology was founded with a simple mission: to empower businesses through cutting-edge technology and innovative solutions. What started as a small team of passionate developers in a garage has grown into a full-service technology company serving clients across industries worldwide. Our headquarters are located in the heart of Silicon Valley, with additional offices in New York and London.

Our Mission

To empower businesses through technology innovation, delivering scalable solutions that drive growth and efficiency while maintaining the highest standards of quality and security. We believe technology should be an enabler, not a barrier, and we strive to build long-term partnerships with our clients.

Our Vision

To be the leading technology partner globally, recognized for transforming businesses through cutting-edge digital solutions and exceptional customer success. We envision a world where every business can leverage technology to achieve its full potential.

Our Values

Team Expertise

Our team comprises 50+ skilled professionals with expertise in software development, cloud architecture, UI/UX design, and digital strategy. We continuously invest in our team's growth and stay current with the latest technology trends. Our leadership team has an average of 10+ years of experience in the technology sector, with a proven track record of delivering successful projects across finance, healthcare, e-commerce, and education sectors.
