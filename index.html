Our Story

Founded in 2015, Nova has grown to become a trusted partner for businesses seeking innovative technology solutions.

Nova Technology was founded with a simple mission: to empower businesses through cutting-edge technology and innovative solutions. What started as a small team of passionate developers has grown into a full-service technology company serving clients across industries worldwide. Our headquarters are located in the heart of Silicon Valley, with additional offices in New York and London. We've successfully completed projects for Fortune 500 companies, emerging startups, and government agencies across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Our Mission

To empower businesses through technology innovation, delivering scalable solutions that drive growth and efficiency while maintaining the highest standards of quality and security. We believe technology should be an enabler, not a barrier, and we strive to build long-term partnerships with our clients. Our approach combines technical expertise with business strategy to deliver solutions that address real-world challenges.

Our Vision

To be the leading technology partner globally, recognized for transforming businesses through cutting-edge digital solutions and exceptional customer success. We envision a world where every business can leverage technology to achieve its full potential. We're committed to sustainability and ethical technology practices, ensuring our innovations benefit both our clients and society.
